How to interpret the results

  • Strict accuracy is the share receiving a complete 4/4 rubric score. The ≥3 threshold also accepts minor omissions.
  • Factual retrieval provides building records or EXIF in 42 of 50 cases. Eight controls require acknowledging missing evidence. It does not measure autonomous web search.
  • Scores come from a text-only GPT-5.1 judge against draft references. The judge does not see the image. Answers are blinded to model identity, but self-judging bias remains.
  • 34 cases reuse prior images; the other 66 still share some locations with earlier data. Neither subset is an independent location holdout.
  • Difficulty is an editorial label. Jev’s predicted difficulty, answerability and checker risk are diagnostics, not measured accuracy.
  • Multiple questions reuse images, and identical model selections share an answer. Results are correlated; they are not 300 independent samples.
